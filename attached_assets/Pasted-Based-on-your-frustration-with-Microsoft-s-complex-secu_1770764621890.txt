Based on your frustration with Microsoft's complex security and specific need for a **web-based editor that can integrate into your ERP**, two main alternatives stand out: **ONLYOFFICE Docs** and **Collabora Online**. Both are designed to be embedded into other applications, bypassing the OAuth and iframe issues you've been facing.

Here's a comparison of your best options:

| Feature | ONLYOFFICE Docs | Collabora Online | Other Notable Option |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Strength** | High compatibility with Microsoft formats; modern UI; strong integration | Enterprise-ready; strong LibreOffice core; customizable & open-source | Excellent MS Office-like UI & compatibility |
| **Licensing** | Open-source core | Open-source | Freemium (ad-supported free version) |
| **Best For** | Teams needing reliable MS Office formatting & clean web integration. | Organizations prioritizing data control, customization, and self-hosting. | Users wanting a near-identical MS Office desktop experience. |
| **Web App (Your Need)** | ✅ Yes, "Docs" is a web suite for integration. | ✅ Yes, designed for online collaborative editing. | ❌ Primarily a desktop suite. |

**Why these solve your problem**:
*   **Direct Integration**: Both offer APIs to embed editors directly into your web application. Users edit documents in a clean, controlled interface without being redirected to another service or battling OAuth pop-ups.
*   **Format Fidelity**: They provide strong compatibility with `.docx` and `.xlsx` formats, which is critical for reliability.
*   **Collaboration**: Both support real-time or paragraph-locked co-editing and commenting within your app.

A third strong contender for excellent format compatibility is **WPS Office**. Its free version offers a very familiar, polished interface that closely mimics modern Microsoft Office. However, it is primarily a **desktop application**, not a web suite designed for integration like ONLYOFFICE Docs or Collabora Online.
